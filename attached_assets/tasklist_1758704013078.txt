Neel Prabhu is an educator, entrepreneur, and seasoned technology leader with over 20 years of experience in education technology, international business, and digital transformation. Under his leadership, TechBeats supports 1,000+ institutions and 2 million+ users, delivering ERP systems, recruitment platforms, survey tools, and other impactful solutions.
